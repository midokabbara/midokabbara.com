the problem

shopify plus merchants often run several stores across countries or brands. existing cross-store reporting was limited. comprehensive multi-store reporting was a top-five pain point for those merchants.

what i shipped

led and shipped multi-store reporting: a unified dashboard, reports and data exploration for shopify plus merchants.

we built it into the existing analytics experience. merchants could look across the business, compare stores and break results down by product or channel.
