the drop

amazon2018–2019fashion new programs
0→1 demand system
global launch

the problem

the drop was amazon's first made-to-order fashion business. customers ordered clothes during a limited-time sale.

we needed to turn customer orders into factory purchase orders and stop taking orders when fabric or manufacturing capacity ran out.

my role

owned the supply-chain product roadmap and led development of the demand-management system. i worked through amazon's existing planning, purchasing and fulfillment systems to find what we could reuse. new development focused on tracking shared capacity and stopping sales when capacity ran out.

also developed a framework for fabric and manufacturing commitments before demand was known.

the outcome

the system enabled the drop to launch globally in june 2019.
