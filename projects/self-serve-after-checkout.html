self-serve after checkout

shopify2026orders & returns
buyerself-serve
market-basedmerchant controls

the problem

buyers need a way to request a cancellation or return. merchants need to decide when those options are available. those rules can differ by market, product or collection.

my role

lead product for the orders & returns domain across multiple teams.

led the cancellation initiative and worked with the teams on market rules, return-window overrides and built-in self-serve controls.

buyer cancellation

we introduced cancellation requests for eligible unfulfilled orders. buyers could request a cancellation from their order in customer accounts, and merchants could review and resolve it.

market rules and exceptions

merchants could set return rules by market and change the return window for a collection, product or variant.

customer accounts

the work also includes moving self-serve controls into the customer accounts editor, with settings by market.

what changed

rules adoption increased 38% and self-serve adoption increased 57% following rollout.
