the pin2 challenge

pwc2012–2015corporate responsibility
0→1 created
global flagship csr

the idea

i created pin2, an entrepreneurship competition for high-school students in kuwait, as a corporate responsibility initiative alongside my client work at pwc.

what i did

designed the program, recruited partners and sponsors, and ran it over three years. sponsors included leading businesses in kuwait and the wharton alumni club of the middle east.

the outcome

pwc adopted pin2 as a global flagship csr initiative and featured it in its 2014 global annual review. the kuwait government joined as a partner, and the program expanded nationally.
