turnaround strategy

the problem

a bankrupt airline wanted to restart operations and attract investors. it needed a business plan that addressed why the earlier business had failed.

my role

managed the engagement and developed the turnaround strategy, business plan and financial model.

we analyzed route utilization, demand and competition to identify a viable operating model. the financial model connected routes, pricing, service and costs so we could assess the business as a whole.

the outcome

the strategy helped the airline secure new investment and restart operations.
