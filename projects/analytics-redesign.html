analytics redesign

shopify2023–2025merchant analytics
all merchants
flexible exploration

the problem

shopify's reports were rigid. merchants could use predefined reports, but had limited ways to explore their own questions or dig into a result.

what i worked on

led product for the merchant-facing analytics experience. we brought dashboards, reports and freeform exploration into a consistent experience, with useful templates as a starting point.

merchants could change an analysis using visual controls or by writing queries in shopifyql, combine data from different areas of their business, and add their own reports to a dashboard.

the outcome

report engagement increased 6% year over year, with 13–20% of users combining metrics across data domains.

the shared analytics foundation accelerated delivery of multi-store reporting, ranking and comparison of top performers, annotations and multi-metric visualizations.
